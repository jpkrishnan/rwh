## The Investment Thesis (in brief)

**What's real**: Ford Pro's commercial vehicle platform (~40% US market share, $6.8B FY2025 EBIT, 879K paid software subscriptions growing 30% YoY, $15B software/services growing 8%/yr) is a genuine narrow moat business. The F-Series' 49-year dominance and Maverick Hybrid success validate the hybrid strategy. At 8.6× forward P/E with a 4.2% yield, Ford is not expensive.

**What's the problem**: Model e burns $4–5B/yr in EBIT through at least 2029 — that's ~$17B more in cumulative equity destruction. This isn't one bad quarter; it's a multi-year math problem. The F-150 Lightning was discontinued after massive investment. Q1 2026's 247% earnings beat was largely $1.3B in one-time tariff refunds; underlying adj EBIT was ~$2.2B. At $14.33, Ford's stock price correctly reflects these offsetting forces — it's at PW EV, not at a discount.

**Entry logic**: The stock becomes interesting at $11–12 (recession scenario, Q2 guide-down, commodity spike) where R/R improves to ~2.5–3.7:1 and the dividend yield hits ~5–5.5%.

## Why This Is a Watch, Not an Initiate

**At $14.33, the math doesn't work in your favor:**

The PW EV is ~$14.50 — essentially at current price. R/R of ~1.2:1 means for every $7.67 of upside (to Bull $22) you bear $6.33 of downside (to Bear $8). That's coin-flip asymmetry. Compare to other holdings in this wiki where R/R exceeds 2:1 or 3:1 — Ford doesn't compete.

The stock needs to trade at ~$11–12 to be genuinely asymmetric. That level would require:
- A recession that compresses truck volumes 20–25%
- A Q2 FY2026 miss and guidance cut
- A commodity spike beyond the current $2B headwind

At those prices, with a 4.2% (or higher) dividend yield as downside protection and the Ford Pro franchise intact, the stock would offer compelling 5-year returns.

**Insider signal is real but not decisive**: William Clay Ford Jr.'s $1.93M purchase at $13.82 in February is the single behavioral signal. The controlling family bought the dip — that's a legitimate signal. But insiders can be wrong, and this doesn't change the fundamental math at $14.33.

## Key Risks to Monitor

1. **Model e losses** — any indication of acceleration beyond $5B/yr → Bearish re-rate
2. **Truck cycle** — recession scenario hits F-Series volume hard; not priced in at current multiples
3. **Tariff escalation** — $1B run-rate today; Section 232 auto parts escalation adds risk
4. **Ford Credit quality** — $130B+ receivables; credit cycle risk is a tail risk
5. **Quality/warranty** — FY2025 record recalls; $1B/yr improvement target is the key near-term lever

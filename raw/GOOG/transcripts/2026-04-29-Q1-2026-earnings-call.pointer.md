# GOOG Q1 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

**Date**: April 29, 2026
**Participants**: Sundar Pichai (CEO), Anat Ashkenazi (CFO), Jim Friedland (IR)

**Primary transcript**: [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/29/alphabet-googl-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
**Replay / IR**: https://abc.xyz/investor/

## Key Themes from Call

### Sundar Pichai — CEO Prepared Remarks
- Gemini 2.5 Pro "state-of-the-art on most benchmarks"; Gemini Flash "best in class speed/efficiency"
- AI Overviews: 1.5B+ users/month as of Q1 2026 (up from 1B at year-end 2025)
- AI Overviews driving Search query volume growth and revenue expansion, not cannibalization
- Search market share: data cited from Evercore ISI showed Google info-retrieval share held ~90% despite ChatGPT rollout
- Google Cloud growth "capacity-constrained" — demand exceeding available GPU/TPU compute (TechCrunch, Apr 29 2026)
- Cloud backlog: $462B (nearly doubled QoQ from ~$250B in Q4 2025)
- TPU 8th generation ("Ironside") deployed in production; Trillium (6th gen TPU) powering external Cloud customers
- Waymo: "several millions" of paid rides; expanding Phoenix, SF, LA; Austin next
- Wiz acquisition: integration underway; expected to be "a few points" margin headwind in 2026

### Anat Ashkenazi — CFO Prepared Remarks
- FY2026 capex guidance: $180–190B (up from $75B in FY2025); driven by AI infra (servers, data centers, networking)
- 2027 capex: "significantly increases" from 2026 level
- Cloud margin 32.9% (vs. 17.8% prior year) — scale leverage + AI workloads priced above commodity compute
- Free cash flow $10.1B in Q1 (capex $35.7B is Q1 alone; FCF will improve as capex peaks)
- Net income $62.6B incl. ~$8B unrealized gain from Waymo/other investment portfolio mark-to-market
- Buyback pace maintained; dividend raised 5% to $0.22/share

### Q&A Highlights
- On antitrust: "We're in the appeals process; confident in our products and user choice" — no quantified revenue impact guidance
- On AI search cannibalization: Pichai directly rebutted — AI Overviews increases engagement and monetization per query
- On Cloud capacity: company building ahead of demand; 2H 2026 capacity expected to catch up
- On Wiz margin headwind: "low single-digit %" through end of 2026; accretive on multi-year view
- On YouTube: Shorts monetization gap narrowing; Shorts ads revenue growing faster than long-form

## Analyst Reactions Post-Call
- Multiple firms raised targets to $400–$500 range; no downgrades
- Key bull thesis reinforced: Cloud acceleration + Search resilience + Gemini 2.5 capability
- Bear case narrowed to antitrust structural remedy execution uncertainty

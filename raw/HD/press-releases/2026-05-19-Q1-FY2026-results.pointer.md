|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total Net Sales | $41.765B | $39.856B | +4.8% | Beat ($41.59B est.) |
| Comparable Sales (Total) | +0.6% | –0.3% | — | Beat |
| Comparable Sales (U.S.) | +0.4% | — | — | — |
| Gross Profit | $13.781B | $13.459B | +2.4% | — |
| Gross Margin | 32.96% (~33.0%) | 33.78% | **–82 bps** | Below ~33.1% guidance |
| Operating Income (GAAP) | $4.981B | $5.133B | –3.0% | — |

**Comp detail**: Ticket +2.2%; transactions –1.3%. Big-ticket (>$1,000) +0.8%. Pro outperformed DIY. "The highest comping part of our Pro business was that complex purchase occasion."

**SRS**: ~$4.0B quarterly revenue; slightly negative comps (roofing weakness); "outperformed competitors by hundreds of basis points" in its verticals. SRS enters HD comp base mid-Q2 FY2026.

**Gross margin miss explanation**: –82 bps YoY primarily from GMS consolidation (GMS acquired Sept 2025; first full quarter in Q1 FY2026), not tariff pressure. Management expects gross margin to recover toward 33.1% FY2026 target in H2 as GMS dilution moderates.

**Store count**: 2,361 at quarter end; 2 new openings.

**Stock reaction**: +2.69% on May 19, 2026.

**No guidance revision despite tariff environment**. Management filed for tariff refunds and expects "significant offset" (amount not disclosed). "Modest price movement in some categories, but it won't be broad-based."

## Key Management Quotes

**CEO Ted Decker**: "Our first quarter results were in line with our expectations. The underlying demand in our business was relatively similar to what we saw throughout fiscal 2025, despite greater consumer uncertainty and housing affordability pressure."

**CEO Ted Decker (on macro)**: "The environment is different than it was 3 months ago, probably a more volatile external environment."

**CFO Richard McPhail (on tariffs)**: "We have filed for those tariff refunds. While we don't disclose the amount...we have assumed that could provide significant offset to costs."

**CFO McPhail (on SRS)**: "SRS performed in line with expectations...they delivered positive total sales growth. SRS took considerable share from other distributors." FY2026 SRS outlook: "mid-single digit positive organic growth for the year."

**CFO McPhail (on consumer)**: Customers remain "very healthy" with strong income and access to $11 trillion in home equity.

**EVP Merchandising William Bastek (on tariff pricing)**: "There will be some modest price movement in some categories, but it won't be broad-based."

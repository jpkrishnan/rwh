# Shareholder Letter Pattern Note — F (Ford Motor Company)

**Pattern**: C — No standalone annual shareholder letter
**Fetch approach**: CEO letter is embedded in the DEF 14A proxy statement and annual report

## Status

Ford Motor Company does not publish a standalone annual shareholder letter (Pattern A or B). CEO Jim Farley's shareholder-addressed commentary appears as the introduction to the annual proxy statement (DEF 14A) filed with the SEC each spring.

## Key Themes from FY2021–FY2025 Management Commentary

**FY2021–2022 (Ford+ launch)**: Reorganization into Pro/Blue/Model e; commitment to $50B EV investment; EV break-even target 2026; "We are all in on EVs" framing

**FY2023 (best adj EBIT year, $10.4B)**: Ford Pro momentum validated; F-Series dominant; warranty costs identified as a significant headwind beginning to emerge

**FY2024 (adj EBIT $10.2B; rising warranty)**: Warranty/quality as the primary drag theme; Ford Pro software growth highlighted; Model e losses acknowledged at $(5.1B)

**FY2025 (adj EBIT $6.8B; $15.5B Q4 charges)**: "The honest price of transformation" — Farley acknowledged full EV strategy reset; Lightning discontinuation; break-even moved to 2029. Pivot to hybrid/EREV/affordable BEV. Cost reduction as FY2026 priority.

**FY2026 (Q1 commentary)**: Ford Pro software as structural moat; "powertrain choice not nameplate proliferation"; UEV 2027 target; EREV development underway; 83% domestic assembly as tariff advantage

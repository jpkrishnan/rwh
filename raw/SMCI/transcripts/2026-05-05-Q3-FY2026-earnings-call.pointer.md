## Key Themes from Call

### Charles Liang — CEO Prepared Remarks
- Revenue miss ($10.2B vs. $12.4B consensus) attributed entirely to "customer site readiness" and supply constraints — demand not the issue; record backlog cited
- Gross margin recovery to 9.9% from 6.3% in Q2 — Q2 margin was distorted by one-time expedite charges on large December deployment
- "Record system backlog" as of March 31, 2026 — specific figure not disclosed
- NVIDIA GPU allocation "unchanged" despite legal proceedings; SMCI remains NVIDIA preferred partner
- DCBBS software revenue: $46M in Q3 alone; trajectory toward >$200M annually within 12–18 months
- Enterprise channel: $2.8B in Q3 (28% of total), up 45% sequentially — sticky relationship business
- DLC leadership: >3,000 liquid-cooled racks/month manufacturing capacity; "70% of new hyperscale AI deployments use SMCI DLC"

### David Weigand — CFO Prepared Remarks
- Q4 gross margin guidance: 8.2%–8.4% — some margin give-back from Q3 but stabilization thesis maintained
- Working capital: large AR balance ($8.4B) expected to collect in Q4 as deployments complete — cash position will improve from $1.3B
- Debt ($8.8B) primarily floating-rate bank lines used to finance inventory procurement; expected to reduce in H2 FY2026 as inventory converts to cash
- Board investigation ongoing: "cooperating fully"; company cannot provide timetable

### Q&A Highlights
- On DOJ indictment: "SMCI has not been charged. We are cooperating with the government. Our export compliance program is being reviewed and strengthened." — no specific disclosures allowed
- On margin recovery timeline: Q4 8.2–8.4% reflects ongoing price competition; management cited DCBBS and software as the path to structurally higher margins
- On FY2027 guidance: not provided; management declined to forecast beyond Q4 on governance uncertainty
- On Nasdaq compliance: company current on all filings; no outstanding compliance notices

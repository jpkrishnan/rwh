# SMCI Q3 FY2026 Earnings Press Release

**Date**: May 5, 2026 (quarter ended March 31, 2026)

## Key Metrics
- Revenue: $10.2B (+123% YoY; –19% QoQ; missed consensus ~$12.4B)
- Gross Margin (GAAP): 9.9% | Non-GAAP: 10.1% (recovery from Q2's 6.3%)
- Operating Income: $626M
- Net Income: $483M
- GAAP Diluted EPS: $0.72 | Non-GAAP: $0.84 (beat $0.62 consensus)
- Basic shares: 600.2M; Diluted shares: 692.2M
- Nine-month FY2026 revenue: $27.9B; Net income: $1.05B; Non-GAAP diluted EPS: $1.89
- Nine-month operating cash flow: –$7.6B (WC build)

## Balance Sheet (March 31, 2026)
- Cash: $1.3B
- Accounts receivable: $8.4B
- Inventories: $11.1B
- Total assets: $23.5B
- Total debt: $8.8B

## Guidance Issued
- Q4 FY2026 Revenue: $11.0B – $12.5B
- Q4 FY2026 GAAP EPS: $0.53 – $0.67
- Q4 FY2026 Non-GAAP EPS: $0.65 – $0.79
- Q4 FY2026 Gross Margin (GAAP): 8.2% – 8.4%

## Key Call Themes
- Revenue miss attributed to customer site readiness delays and supply constraints, not demand
- "Record backlog" cited by management
- NVIDIA GPU allocation unchanged despite legal proceedings
- Enterprise channel revenue $2.8B (28% of total), up 45% sequentially
- DCBBS software revenue $46M in Q3 alone
- Management: DCBBS will contribute >25% of total profit "soon"

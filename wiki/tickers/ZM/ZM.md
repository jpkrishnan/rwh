## Business Overview

Zoom Communications, Inc. (NASDAQ: ZM; legal name adopted November 25, 2024, formerly Zoom Video Communications) operates an AI-first unified communications and collaboration platform. Founded in 2011 by CEO Eric Yuan — a former Webex founding engineer — Zoom IPO'd April 2019 and became a global verb during COVID-19 before revenue growth decelerated sharply as workplaces reopened. Fiscal year ends January 31.

Today, Zoom markets under the **Zoom Workplace** umbrella (rebranded April 2024), unifying Meetings, Phone, Contact Center, AI Companion, Webinars, Events, Rooms, Team Chat, Workvivo (employee experience, acquired 2023), Docs, Mail, and Calendar. Revenue is generated via per-seat monthly/annual subscriptions in two segments: **Enterprise** (~60% of FY2026 revenue, +6.5% YoY) and **Online** (~40%, +1.2% YoY). The business is structurally capital-light: 77% gross margins, 40.4% non-GAAP operating margins, $1.9B annual FCF on $4.9B revenue, with zero financial debt and $7.8B net cash.

## Section 3 — Competitive Moat & Landscape

**Moat Rating: Narrow** — strong meetings brand and growing multi-product switching costs, but structurally challenged by Microsoft's bundle economics.

**Sources of moat**:
- **Brand and habit**: "Zoom" is a global verb; 56% estimated video conferencing share reflects deep habitual use across healthcare, education, legal, and enterprise external communications ([Market.us](https://scoop.market.us/video-conferencing-statistics/))
- **Multi-product lock-in**: Meetings + Phone + Contact Center customers face meaningful migration friction (number porting, hardware, training, API integrations). Fortune 10 customers on 140,000+ Zoom Phone seats have high switching costs.
- **Ecosystem openness**: Integrates with M365, Google Workspace, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Atlassian — not captive to one ecosystem; this is a genuine differentiator vs. Teams
- **AI Companion depth**: AI Companion 3.0 (December 2025) with agentic workflows and federated knowledge retrieval is ahead of Teams Copilot for meeting-centric AI *as of early 2026*
- **Gartner recognition**: Magic Quadrant Leader in UCaaS sixth consecutive year ([news.zoom.com](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-2025-gartner-magic-quadrant-ucaas/)); also a CCaaS Leader simultaneously — one of only two vendors achieving both. [Forrester UCaaS Wave 2025 Leader](https://news.zoom.com/forrester-wave-2025-ucaas/).

**Moat vulnerabilities**:
- Low switching costs for meetings-only customers (Teams/Meet are viable substitutes at near-zero incremental cost for M365/Workspace users)
- Microsoft Copilot investment dwarfs Zoom's AI R&D budget
- Online segment has no meaningful switching costs (month-to-month)
- AI-native meeting tools (Otter.ai, Fireflies, Fathom) encroach on AI Companion from below

## Section 5 — Strategic Growth Initiatives

### A. Zoom AI Companion (Platform Intelligence Layer)

The central strategic initiative. Bundled free with all paid Zoom Workplace plans as a retention/upsell catalyst, with two paid tiers:
- **Standalone** ($10/user/month): Free-plan users wanting AI features
- **Custom AI Companion** ($12/user/month): Cross-platform note-taking (Google Meet, Teams), custom templates, enterprise knowledge retrieval, personal AI coach, agentic workflow automation

**AI Companion 3.0** (December 15, 2025): Low-code "personal workflows" designer with drag-and-drop automation; federated knowledge retrieval across Google Drive, OneDrive, Jira; agentic writing canvas at ai.zoom.us. **March 2026 expansion**: AI Companion 3.0 launched in Zoom Workplace app (not just web); "Zoomie" AI facilitator across Rooms, Meetings, Chat.

**Adoption**: MAU more than tripled YoY in Q4 FY2026; side-panel usage more than doubled QoQ; all 10 of Q4's top-10 enterprise deals included paid AI. No ARR figure publicly disclosed — the **May 21 earnings call** is the first expected opportunity for disclosure.

### ⚠️ Risk 1: Microsoft Teams Structural Ceiling

Microsoft Teams remains the most material competitive risk. Despite EU-forced unbundling (June 2024), Microsoft removed EA volume discounts (November 1, 2025), partially restoring its procurement advantage ([SamExpert](https://samexpert.com/teams-rebundling-pricing-2025/)). Most large enterprise M365 customers access Teams at near-zero marginal cost. Zoom's 10-K explicitly acknowledges competitors offering bundled platforms "at low or no incremental cost." **Thesis-break trigger**: Enterprise NDE rate falls below 90% for two consecutive quarters.

### ⚠️ Risk 2: Revenue Growth Plateau / Deceleration

FY2027 guidance implies ~4.1% growth. Seven consecutive quarters of 3–5% revenue growth. Without AI monetization or Phone/CCaaS breaking through, Zoom is a buyback-driven EPS story with slowly declining revenue trajectory — not a re-rating candidate. FY2023–FY2024 NRR decline (130% → 99% → 98%) signals persistent enterprise spending pressure. **Thesis-break trigger**: Revenue growth decelerates below 2% for two consecutive quarters.

### ⚠️ Risk 3: AI Monetization Uncertainty (Not Priced In)

Growth re-acceleration depends on Custom AI Companion ($12/user/month) and ZCX AI becoming material contributors. In FY2026, AI monetization was primarily indirect. FY2027 is declared the "monetization year" but no ARR baseline has been disclosed. Risks: (a) customers resist $12/month add-on atop base license; (b) Microsoft Copilot commoditizes AI meeting features; (c) AI-native point tools (Otter.ai, Fireflies) undercut from below; (d) generative AI creates liability risk (hallucinations in meeting summaries, privacy concerns).

### ⚠️ Risk 4: FY2027 FCF Step-Down

FY2027 FCF guided $1.72B (midpoint) vs. $1.924B FY2026 = −10.5%. Management attributed to AI infrastructure investment and higher cash taxes. If this is structural (not one-time), the FCF compounder thesis loses force. **Thesis-break trigger**: FCF declines >15% YoY.

### ⚠️ Risk 5: Online Segment Churn Acceleration

Online revenue (40% of total) at 2.9%/month churn and only +1.2% YoY. Structural headwinds: COVID-era individual subscriptions churning; Teams/Meet bundled in enterprise suites; RTO reducing individual home-office Zoom use. If Online resumes decline to −3–4%/year (as in FY2023–FY2024), total company revenue growth stalls at 3–4% or below. **Trigger**: Online monthly churn rises above 4% for two consecutive quarters.

### ⚠️ Risk 6: Data Privacy / AI Regulatory

Zoom processes hundreds of millions of sensitive communications. FY2026 10-K flags: GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws; EU AI Act implications for AI Companion's meeting content processing; AI training data liability (2023 ToS controversy precedent); potential product restrictions in regulated industries. Source: [FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1585521/000158552126000030/zm-20260131.htm).

### ⚠️ Risk 7: Founder/Governance Concentration

Yuan's >70% voting control is simultaneously a strategic asset and governance risk. The dual-class structure means institutional holders cannot force accountability on major decisions. Recent C-suite churn (President "termination without cause," CFO replaced, COO resigned May 2026) has not been fully explained to shareholders. Geopolitical: Yuan's Chinese origin and Zoom's historical engineering concentration have drawn U.S. government scrutiny. Source: [FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1585521/000158552126000030/zm-20260131.htm).
